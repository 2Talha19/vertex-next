# Peham Internship — Quick Handbook (sample)

## What this internship is for
The Peham AI Developer Internship teaches you to build real AI products — not only chat in a browser.
You learn to call LLMs from code, ground answers in private documents (RAG), use tools carefully, automate workflows (n8n), and ship a capstone for Peham (Ask Peham's Docs).
Goal: leave able to explain and demo a working internal AI tool on your CV.

## Office hours
Mentor office hours: Tuesday and Thursday, 3:00–5:00 PM (PKT), online via Meet.

## How we work
- One exercise at a time unless told otherwise
- Keep API keys in `.env` / `.env.local` — never commit secrets
- Daily notes go in `internship-logbook/`

## Stack (target)
Next.js, TypeScript, Supabase (pgvector), Groq, Docker/Coolify for deploy
