# Peham — Onboarding FAQ (sample)

Q: What is the internship for?
A: To train AI developers who can ship internal tools: APIs, RAG over company docs, light agents/tools, and a deployed capstone useful to Peham.

Q: What is the capstone?
A: Weeks 4–5: ship one internal tool (Ask Peham's Docs). Must include RAG, a tool or structured step, auth (later), and deploy.

Q: What is RAG?
A: Retrieval-Augmented Generation — search your documents, then let the LLM answer using those chunks, with citations. The user gets a normal answer; similarity search only happens behind the scenes.

Q: Support vs Docs?
A: Docs = ask company knowledge. Support triage = classify tickets + human approve (optional second project).
